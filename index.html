A Lost Decade, A Fragile Turnaround — and a Bet on the World's First Aeroponic Coffee Platform

失落的十年、脆弱的拐点 —— 以及一项全球首创雾化水培咖啡平台的战略投资

Regal Hotels earned HK$2.3bn cumulatively in 2016–2019, then gave it all back several times over: 2020–2025 losses total HK$-7.3bn. The 2026 interim profit of HK$158m — driven by a one-off hotel disposal — marks the first positive inflection in six years. This report deconstructs the ten-year trajectory and evaluates whether a minimal-capital aeroponic coffee program, funded entirely from asset-recycling proceeds, can convert 14 captive hotels into a 73.5%-margin recurring business and begin closing a 97% discount to adjusted NAV.

富豪酒店2016–2019累计盈利约23亿港元，2020–2025累计亏损却达约73亿港元——十年净亏约50亿。2026年中期录得1.58亿港元盈利（主要由出售富豪东方酒店一次性收益驱动），是六年来首个正向拐点。本报告拆解十年轨迹，并评估：以资产循环所得全额资助、不新增杠杆的雾化水培咖啡计划，能否把14家自有/管理酒店转化为73.5%毛利率的经常性业务，开始收窄对经调整NAV高达97%的折让。

Executive Summary核心结论

📉 Where the Group Stands: Cash-Positive, Equity-Poor

📉 集团现状：现金为正、权益单薄

Accounting losses are dominated by non-cash items: HK$575m/yr hotel depreciation, fair-value swings, and the one-off Cosmopolitan write-down. Underlying cash generation is real — FY2025 CFO of HK$1.36bn (49% of revenue) — but the equity cushion has halved since 2022 and net debt of HK$11.9bn caps any levered expansion. Deleveraging via asset recycling (Regal Oriental: HK$1.52bn, ~HK$0.8bn gain in FY2026) is the only viable capital source.

会计亏损由非现金项目主导：每年约5.75亿港元酒店折旧、公平值波动、四海国际一次性减值。底层现金创造真实存在——FY2025经营现金流13.6亿港元（占收入49%）——但归母权益自2022年近腰斩，119亿净债务封死了加杠杆扩张的空间。资产循环（富豪东方：物业价值15.2亿港元，FY2026确认约8亿收益）是唯一可行的资本来源。

🧬 The Recommendation: US$10m, Three Phases, Zero New Leverage

🧬 投资建议：三阶段合计1,000万美元，零新增杠杆

Deploy ≈HK$78m (US$10m) over five years — just 0.5% of adjusted NAV — funded wholly from disposal proceeds. Unit model: US$414k per farm, 615k seedlings/yr, US$2.26m revenue at a 73.5% gross margin, payback 2–3 years, IRR ≈25–35%. The differentiator vs any peer: Regal's 14 hotels are a captive market for house-brand coffee with zero customer-acquisition cost.

五年分期投入约7,800万港元（US$1,000万）——仅占经调整NAV约0.5%，资金全部来自资产出售所得。单农场模型：投入US$41.4万，年产61.5万株，年收入US$226万，毛利率73.5%，回收期2–3年，IRR约25–35%。相对同业的关键差异：富豪旗下14家酒店是自有品牌咖啡的内嵌市场，获客成本为零。

📊 The Payoff: From Disposal-Driven to Recurring

📊 回报：从处置驱动到经常性盈利

The 2026H1 turnaround leans on a one-off gain. A coffee segment at 8–10% of revenue lifts blended gross margin (29.4% in FY2025) by ~3–4pct with three rolling harvest cycles a year — the recurring earnings layer the equity story lacks, and a credible first step toward narrowing a ~97% discount to adjusted NAV.

2026中期的扭亏依赖一次性收益。若咖啡分部达到总营收8–10%，可将整体毛利率（FY2025为29.4%）提升约3–4个百分点，且每年3批滚动产出——这正是当前股权故事缺失的经常性盈利层，也是收窄约97%经调整NAV折让的第一步。
