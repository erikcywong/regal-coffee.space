How Much Should Regal Invest? US$10 Million — Funded Entirely by Asset Recycling, Never by Borrowing.

富豪应投多少？1,000万美元 —— 全部来自资产循环，绝不动用新增借贷。

With net debt of HK$11.9bn and a market capitalization of just HK$413m, Regal cannot afford leverage-funded ventures. But it is already harvesting HK$1.5bn+ per disposal. Allocating 0.5% of adjusted NAV (US$10m over five years) to aeroponic coffee — one farm to validate, five to commercialize, fifteen to scale — converts a fraction of recycling proceeds into a perpetual 73.5%-margin earnings layer with a 2–3 year payback.

净债务119亿港元、市值仅4.13亿港元的富豪，承受不起借贷驱动的多元化。但它每一笔资产处置即回笼15亿港元以上。将经调整NAV的0.5%（五年1,000万美元）配置给雾培咖啡——1个农场验证、5个商业化、15个规模化——把一小部分处置所得，转化为永续的73.5%毛利率盈利层，回收期仅2–3年。

4.4

Why This Size — and Only This Size为什么是这个金额——也只能是这个金额

1 · No leverage headroom

1 · 没有加杠杆的空间

Net gearing reached ~206% (2025) and net debt is HK$11.9bn. Any venture funded by new borrowing would raise the cost of capital exactly when HIBOR relief is doing the heavy lifting. The program must be — and can be — funded entirely from recycling proceeds (one disposal ≈ HK$1.5bn; the whole 5-year program is ~5% of that).

净负债率2025年约206%、净债务119亿港元。任何以新增借贷资助的业务，都会在HIBOR下行正发力的当口推高资金成本。本计划必须——且能够——完全由资产循环所得资助（单笔处置≈15亿港元；五年计划总额约为其5%）。

2 · Optionality: US$0.5m buys the answer

2 · 期权价值：US$50万买到答案

Phase 0 is a cheap real option: US$0.5m resolves whether the 73.5% margin holds inside hotel spaces. If it does, phases 1–2 proceed; if not, the write-off equals ~0.2% of adjusted NAV. Asymmetric payoff, capped downside — the correct structure for a group whose equity base has already halved since 2022.

Phase 0是一张廉价的实物期权：US$50万即可验证73.5%毛利率能否在酒店空间内成立。成立则推进Phase 1–2；不成立则核销额约为经调整NAV的0.2%。收益不对称、下行封顶——对一家权益自2022年已腰斩的集团，这是唯一正确的结构。

3 · Disclosure discipline

3 · 披露纪律

With a market cap of just HK$413m, even US$10m is material to optics. Each phase must be announced, milestone-gated and reported as a separate segment from day one — transparency is the re-rating mechanism itself, given the ~97% NAV discount rests on a credibility deficit.

市值仅4.13亿港元，即使US$1,000万在观感上也是重大投入。每一阶段必须公告、设里程碑门槛，并从第一天起独立分部列报——鉴于约97%的NAV折让本质是信用赤字，透明本身就是重估机制。
