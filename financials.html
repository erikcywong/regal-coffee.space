Steady Profits Wiped Out — and a Turnaround That Rests on One Asset Sale

稳健盈利归零 —— 以及一个建立在单一资产出售上的转折

2016–2019 delivered HK$22bn of revenue and HK$2.2bn of cumulative profit. COVID cut 2020 revenue by 69.5% overnight; 2023–2024 added fair-value losses and a HK$946m Cosmopolitan write-down. Cumulative 2020–2025 losses of ~HK$7.3bn erased the prior five years' profits several times over. FY2025 revenue hit a decade high of HK$2.76bn (+51.1%) yet still lost HK$1.2bn — and the 2026 interim profit of HK$158m is driven by a one-off disposal gain, not yet by core operations.

2016–2019年累计营收约220亿、盈利约22亿港元。疫情令2020年收入一夜暴跌69.5%；2023–2024年叠加公平值损失与四海国际9.46亿减值。2020–2025累计亏损约73亿港元，数倍吞没此前五年利润。FY2025营收创下十年新高27.6亿港元（+51.1%）却仍亏12亿港元——而2026中期1.58亿港元盈利由一次性出售收益驱动，核心经营尚未真正转正。

Analyst Read分析师解读

Depreciation distorts the P&L

折旧扭曲损益表

Hong Kong hotel properties carry HK$575m of annual depreciation — more than the entire hotel segment profit of most years. Accounting losses overstate economic deterioration; CFO has been positive every year since 2021 and reached HK$1.36bn in FY2025. Judge this company on EBITDA and cash flow, not reported profit.

香港自有酒店物业每年计提折旧约5.75亿港元——超过多数年份酒店分部全部溢利。会计亏损夸大了经济恶化程度；经营现金流自2021年以来每年为正，FY2025达13.6亿港元。评估这家公司应看EBITDA与现金流，而非报表利润。

Core hotel profit is razor-thin

酒店主业利润薄如刀刃

The hotel segment — 66.8% of revenue — earned just HK$15.6m of segment profit in FY2025, a margin below 0.1%. RevPAR growth (+9.5% Regal, +13.6% iclub) is real but the cost and funding base consumes it all. The Group needs a second recurring profit engine with structurally higher margins.

占总营收66.8%的酒店分部，FY2025分部溢利仅1,560万港元，利润率不足0.1%。RevPAR增长（富豪+9.5%、富荟+13.6%）是真实的，但成本与资金基础将其消耗殆尽。集团需要一个利润率结构性更高的第二经常性利润引擎。

2026H1: a real inflection, but read the footnote

2026中期：真实的拐点，但要读脚注

The +HK$158m interim profit rests on the HK$849m gain from selling the Regal Oriental Hotel. Stripping it, operations improved (EBITDA positive, finance costs −22.6% on falling HIBOR, REIT core operations back to profit) but are not yet self-sustaining at scale. The recovery needs more recurring profit, not more disposals.

中期+1.58亿港元盈利建立在出售富豪东方酒店的8.5亿港元收益之上。剔除后经营确有改善（EBITDA转正、HIBOR下行令融资成本−22.6%、REIT核心经营恢复盈利），但尚未形成规模化自我维持。复苏需要更多经常性利润，而非更多资产出售。
