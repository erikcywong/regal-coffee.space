From Cost Structure to Industrial Innovation — Inside a Hotel Group

从成本结构到产业化创新 —— 而且就长在酒店集团里

The NAPELL Aeroponic Coffee Cultivation System replaces soil-based nurseries with a precision nutrient-mist pipeline: a 4-month growth cycle, three harvests per year, year-round output under controlled climate. For Regal, the decisive variable is distribution: a 73.5%-margin product sold into the Group's own 14 hotels, F&B outlets and lounges — a captive market no standalone agtech venture can replicate.

NAPELL雾化水培咖啡种植系统以精密营养雾管道取代土壤苗圃：4个月生长周期、年产3批、可控气候下全年产出。对富豪而言，决定性变量是渠道：73.5%毛利率的产品，卖进集团自有14家酒店、餐饮与行政酒廊——这是任何独立农业科技创业公司都无法复制的内嵌市场。

The climate thesis

气候逻辑

ICO research projects global land suitable for coffee could contract by up to 50% by 2050, threatening the US$200bn+ coffee economy and 125 million livelihoods. Every displaced hectare creates seedling demand; every climate shock accelerates replanting with resilient, tissue-cultured stock. Demand here is climate-driven — uncorrelated with Hong Kong hotel RevPAR or the mainland-traveler spending shift.

ICO研究预测，气候压力下2050年全球适宜咖啡种植的土地面积可能缩减高达50%，威胁2,000亿美元以上的咖啡经济与1.25亿人生计。每一公顷退出的种植地都在创造幼苗需求；每一次气候冲击都在加速抗逆组培种苗的补种。这里的需求由气候驱动——与香港酒店RevPAR或北上消费迁移完全无关。

Why This Fits Regal's Asset Base为何契合富豪的资产禀赋

14 hotels = built-in distribution

14家酒店 = 自带渠道

Five Regal hotels, four iclub hotels via Regal REIT (74.9% owned), Regala Skycity at the airport, plus managed properties — every lobby, lounge and banquet is a sales point for house-brand coffee and for seedlings to regional buyers. First tranche of output sells at zero customer-acquisition cost.

透过富豪产业信托（持股74.9%）持有的5家富豪酒店与4家富荟酒店、机场Regala Skycity，及管理酒店——每一家大堂、酒廊与宴会厅都是自有品牌咖啡和区域买家幼苗的销售点。首批产出零获客成本。

Spaces that earn nothing today

今天不产生收入的空间

Hotel podiums, rooftops and back-of-house plots across the portfolio can host greenhouses without land acquisition — removing the largest capex item in the unit model. Airport-corridor sites (Regala Skycity, Terminal 2 now operational) suit agri-tourism add-ons for transit guests.

组合内酒店的裙楼、天台与后场地块可改建温室而无需新购土地——直接削减单位模型中最大的资本开支项。机场走廊物业（Regala Skycity，二号客运大楼已启用）适合为中转旅客增设农旅体验。

A REIT built for green money

为绿色资金而生的REIT

Regal REIT (01881) is a listed vehicle whose funding cost already swings with HIBOR. A quantifiable carbon-data asset and a climate-resilience narrative position the REIT for green loans and sustainability-linked refinancing — every 100bp saved on the Group's HK$14.6bn debt is ~HK$146m.

富豪产业信托（01881）是上市平台，融资成本本就随HIBOR波动。可量化的碳数据资产与气候韧性叙事，为REIT争取绿色贷款与可持续发展挂钩再融资创造条件——集团146亿港元债务每降100个基点≈1.46亿港元。
