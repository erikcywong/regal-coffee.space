A HK$15bn Debt Mountain, A Halved Equity Base — and Cash Flow That Keeps Breathing

150亿债务之山、腰斩的权益 —— 以及仍在呼吸的现金流

Interest-bearing debt climbed from HK$6.5bn (2016) to a peak of HK$16.3bn (2023), standing at HK$14.6bn at end-2025 with net gearing of ~206%. Attributable equity has halved since 2022 to HK$5.3bn. Yet operating cash flow has never turned negative — HK$1.36bn in FY2025 — and the asset-recycling program (Regal Oriental at HK$1.52bn) is finally bending the curve: net debt down to HK$11.9bn by 2026H1, gearing 54.3%.

有息债务从64.9亿港元（2016）一路攀升至162.9亿峰值（2023），2025年末仍达146.1亿港元，净负债率约206%。归母权益自2022年腰斩至53亿港元。但经营现金流从未转负——FY2025达13.6亿港元——而资产循环计划（富豪东方物业价值15.2亿港元）终于使曲线弯折：2026H1净债务降至119.5亿港元，负债比率54.3%。

Analyst Read分析师解读

0.08× book — 0.03× adjusted NAV

0.08倍账面 —— 0.03倍经调整NAV

At HK$0.46, the stock trades at 8% of book NAV (HK$5.89) and ~3% of the company's own market-value-adjusted NAV (HK$16.70 per share). Even granting the market deep skepticism about hotel values and leverage, this prices near-total equity impairment — the re-rating math is extreme if credibility is rebuilt.

股价0.46港元，仅为账面NAV（5.89港元）的8%、公司自身按市值重列后经调整NAV（16.70港元/股）的约3%。即使市场对酒店估值与杠杆抱有极深怀疑，这一价格也接近于股权全额减值——一旦信用重建，重估空间在数学上是极端的。

Deleveraging is real but bought, not earned

去杠杆是真的，但是买来的，不是赚来的

Debt reduction (net debt HK$11.9bn at 2026H1) is funded by selling hotels — Regal Oriental for HK$1.52bn — i.e., shrinking the asset base to shrink the liability base. Sustainable deleveraging needs an earnings engine that services debt from operations. That is precisely what a recurring agri-income stream would begin to provide.

降债（2026H1净债务119.5亿港元）靠出售酒店实现——富豪东方作价15.2亿港元——即以收缩资产端来收缩负债端。可持续的去杠杆需要一个能用经营利润偿债的盈利引擎。这正是一项经常性农业收入流能够开始提供的。

HIBOR is the hidden tailwind

HIBOR是隐性顺风

Finance costs fell 22.6% in FY2025 to HK$769m and net interest fell to HK$310m by 2026H1 — rate relief alone contributed as much to the turnaround as operations did. Every 100bp on HK$14.6bn of debt is ~HK$146m: cheaper green refinancing would be a second, structural rate cut.

FY2025融资成本下降22.6%至7.69亿港元，2026H1净利息支出降至3.10亿港元——仅利率下行对扭亏的贡献就与经营改善相当。146亿港元债务每降100个基点≈1.46亿港元：更便宜的绿色再融资相当于第二次、结构性的降息。
